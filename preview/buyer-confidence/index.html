What the proposed customer experience covers

One purchase. Five decisions.

For Whole, Flat and Point raw brisket. The interactive exercise below demonstrates one decision; it is not the full five-module R3 prototype.

01 / CONFIRMKnow the cutUse the actual product label and check the condition before proceeding.
02 / IDENTIFYFind the grainRecord a reliable fibre-direction cue before the bark hides it.
03 / DECIDECheck the trimSkip unnecessary trimming; Whole-only corrections require a clear reason.
04 / MONITORCook by checkpointsSeparate equipment, safety and tenderness decisions; wording requires buyer approval.
05 / FINISHSlice across fibresRe-check the actual grain direction before slicing each muscle.
