Proposed B2B pilot · Raw brisket education

Help customers cook the brisket they actually bought.

CarniStore’s raw Beef Brisket page offers “Braise or Smoke,” while its visible method explains braising. WASLAH proposes a mobile smoking guide linked to actual Whole, Flat and Point products—focused on practical decisions after delivery, not another general BBQ recipe.

Why this matters: potential product-specific customer guidance where a generic free recipe does not know what the buyer ordered. The opportunity is a hypothesis, not a proven conversion or support benefit.
What is included

A focused five-module journey

Know what you bought
Find the grain before cooking
Check trim (Whole only)
Cook by decisions
Re-find grain, then slice

English mobile-first static web delivery. Whole, 1 kg Flat and 1 kg Point; Cubes excluded. Five custom instructional illustrations, five decision cards, one single-question check per module (optional in buyer flow), up to three retailer-selected product links, QR/post-purchase copy and a four-event analytics specification. One consolidated factual/safety and brand review cycle.

Why pay instead of linking a free recipe?

Specific to the purchased product

1 · Exact raw-cut decisions

Guide customers from a checked product label through their actual cut, with explicit distinction from ready-to-eat Smoked Brisket.

2 · A deployable retailer asset

CarniStore-approved wording, illustrations, product hand-offs and copy for the selected placement—not an unattributed recipe link.

3 · Measurable, without fake ROI

Observe guide opens, stages viewed, guide completion and product-link clicks using retailer-connected event hooks.

4 · Clear approval boundaries

CarniStore owns approval of SKU facts, safety language, brand voice and deployment. No unapproved cooking certification claims.
