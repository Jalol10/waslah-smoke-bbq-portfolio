BBQ Beef AcademyA privately developed instructional web prototype. The work explores how complex brisket skills can become a clear sequence of visual observations and small learner decisions.
01 / THE DESIGN PROBLEM

Knowledge is everywhere.
Clarity is not.

For a first-time learner, even a useful technique becomes difficult when the visual target, the action and the checkpoint are buried in dense explanation.

  • One clear observation SEE
  • One meaningful next step DECIDE
  • One immediate verification CHECK
01 / THE APPROACH

A modular, reviewable learning experience.

Responsive lesson paths, focused illustrations, decision cues and immediate feedback. Built as a private product prototype; not presented as a commercially validated course.

THE DESIGN METHOD

Less friction.
More understanding.

Three simple principles guide the build. Every screen should reduce guesswork and make the next decision more legible.

01 / DEFINE

One clear job.

Choose a single learner action, define the starting conditions and make the expected result explicit.

02 / DESIGN

Show, then tell.

Put the visual target next to the instruction. Use restrained interfaces that work on mobile screens.

03 / VERIFY

Make it testable.

Include a short check and prepare the experience for independent review before external release.
